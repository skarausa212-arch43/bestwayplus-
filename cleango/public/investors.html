LUMI · Investor Overview

The Home Operating System

LUMI is evolving from a cleaning marketplace into one platform for booking, managing and maintaining residential properties.

Problem

Home maintenance is fragmented across dozens of disconnected providers — hard to book, price, trust and track.

Solution

One platform for booking, managing and maintaining a home: instant dispatch, transparent AI pricing, and a living digital record of every property.

Revenue model

Marketplace commission — a platform fee on every completed job (never exposed to customers or providers).
Premium subscriptions — LUMI+ recurring revenue with member discounts and perks.
Corporate plans — companies and property managers.
Partner ecosystem — future: appliances, insurance, IoT.
