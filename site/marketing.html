Approach

Restraint is a commercial asset.

A great deal of football marketing is volume: more posts, more content, more visibility. Serious sponsors are not buying volume. They are buying credibility, consistency and an audience that actually believes the athlete.

We build slowly and in the athlete's own voice, in the languages that matter for their market. For younger players, we are usually arguing for less exposure rather than more.

Where a player is represented, commercial arrangements are agreed through their licensed agent.
