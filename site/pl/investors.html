Position

What we are, and what we are not.

We are a consulting and coordination company. We find and prepare opportunities, we do the groundwork that makes them assessable, and we introduce the parties who should be talking to each other.

We are not an investment adviser, a broker or a fund. We do not solicit investment, we do not promise returns, and we do not present opportunities as recommendations. Regulated advice, valuation opinions and audit work are performed by the appropriate licensed professionals.

That distinction matters more in football than in most sectors, because the sector attracts a great deal of confident language and very little documentation.
