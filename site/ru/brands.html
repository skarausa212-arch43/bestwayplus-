Where a European football partnership makes sense

Football gives a brand access to audiences that are difficult to buy elsewhere: young, international, concentrated and emotionally engaged. It also gives them to competitors, at scale, badly targeted.

Our work is narrowing that. A regional brand entering a new market usually needs a specific club and a specific set of players, not a category-wide campaign. A consumer brand wants credibility with an audience that detects insincerity immediately.

We prepare both sides properly and negotiate on brief — and where a player is represented, we work through their licensed agent.
