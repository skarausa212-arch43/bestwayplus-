Most football projects fail on ordinary business grounds, not football ones.

Underestimated running costs, revenue assumptions borrowed from a different market, governance that cannot survive a bad season, and partners chosen because they were available rather than because they were right.

Our work is deliberately unromantic. We research the market, examine the assumptions, and set out what the project would need in order to work — including, when it applies, the conclusion that it will not.
