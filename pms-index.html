Frequently asked questions

Everything about how PayMeSafe secures your deals. Can't find an answer? Start a deal or reach us anytime.

What is PayMeSafe?+
PayMeSafe is a P2P escrow platform. It holds the payment in a secure crypto vault until both sides fulfil the agreed conditions — so no one has to trust a stranger with their money. Any kind of deal, any country: funds move only by the rules you agreed on.
How does an escrow deal work?+
Four simple steps: 1) create a deal and pick a category, 2) invite your counterparty by a secure link, 3) the buyer funds the escrow — money is locked, visible to both, spendable by neither, 4) once the conditions are met the funds are released to the seller. If something goes wrong, either side can open a dispute for arbitration.
What can I trade through PayMeSafe?+
Practically anything: 💰 Crypto & OTC, 🚗 Vehicles, 🏠 Real estate, 💼 Business & shares, 📦 Goods, 💻 Digital assets (domains, websites, apps, channels, NFTs), 🌍 International trade, and ⭐ any custom deal. You choose the exact type when creating a deal.
What currency is held in escrow?+
Deals are secured in USDT on the TRON network (TRC-20) — fast, low fees, stable value. The escrow is on-chain, so the locked amount is verifiable at any time.
What are the fees?+
Total platform fee is 2%. You choose who covers it — the buyer, the seller, or split 50/50 (1% each). You always see the exact numbers before locking any funds. The network (TRON) fee is charged by the blockchain itself and doesn't depend on us.
Is my money safe?+
Funds are locked in escrow and released strictly according to the conditions both parties agreed on — never unilaterally. Every movement is recorded on-chain. Note: the platform is currently in a pilot / DEV stage; before handling real client funds at scale it goes through a security audit and the required licensing.
What happens if there's a dispute?+
Either side can open a dispute from the deal page. A platform arbiter then reviews the deal chat and the evidence both parties submitted, and decides how the escrow is split (fully to the seller, refunded to the buyer, or a partial split). The decision is applied automatically to the locked funds.
How do I invite my counterparty?+
When you create a deal you get a unique secure invite link to send your partner. They open it, join the deal, and both of you see the same live status and chat.
Do I need to pass KYC?+
No KYC at the MVP stage — registration is a free, super-fast couple of clicks. You confirm your email to activate the account, add a payout wallet, and you're ready to deal.
Can I get a refund?+
Yes. If the agreed conditions aren't met, or an arbiter rules in your favour, the escrowed amount is returned to the payout wallet on your account — that's exactly why we ask for your TRON address at sign-up.
