§01
Services

Three jobs, done on the ground

An agent 2 000 km away can send an e-mail. He cannot sit in a sporting director's office on a Tuesday morning, drive a player to a trial, or stand in a queue at the voivodeship office. That is the part we do.

01 / Marketing

Make the player visible to the twelve people who matter

Not a mass mailing. A dossier built the way Polish clubs read them, put in front of a shortlist we choose with you.

  • Bilingual dossier (PL/EN) — position profile, physical data, minutes played, contract status, salary expectation.
  • Highlight reel cut by position — 6–8 minutes for scouts, 90 seconds for the first message, full matches on request.
  • Data one-pager — Wyscout / InStat / Transfermarkt cleaned up into the numbers a Polish analyst asks for.
  • Presence in Polish — local media, club channels, social profiles that a fan and a director can actually read.
02 / Recruiting

Access, not a list of addresses

We match the player to real squad needs and budgets, then organise everything a trial needs so the club only has to watch him play.

  • Club shortlist — by position need, budget band, playing style and squad-composition rules for the current season.
  • Trials organised end to end — dates, flights, accommodation, insurance, transport, translator, guaranteed pitch time.
  • Two-way flow — we also source Polish players and coaches for clubs your agency works with abroad.
  • Academies and CLJ — placements for 16–19-year-olds, with the schooling and guardianship questions answered first.
03 / Contracts

The part that ends in a signature

Negotiation support, a contract read by a Polish lawyer, and the registration chain that makes the player eligible to step on the pitch.

  • Term sheet & negotiation — salary, bonuses, apartment, car, flights home, release clause, sell-on percentage.
  • Legal review — the contract is checked against Polish labour and PZPN rules by a cooperating law firm before anyone signs.
  • Registration chain — ITC through FIFA TMS, PZPN licensing paperwork, deadlines tracked against the window.
  • Money that arrives — commission split agreed in writing with you up front, invoicing and payment flow handled on the Polish side.
04 / Landing

The paperwork that quietly kills deals

A signed contract is not a settled player. Most transfers that collapse late collapse here.

  • Immigration — national D visa, work permit type A, temporary residence card, family members included.
  • Basics — PESEL number, bank account, tax registration, health insurance, phone and address.
  • Living — apartment near the training ground, car, school for the children, Polish lessons twice a week.
  • Somebody to call — one Polish-speaking contact on the ground for the player and for you, in your time zone.

§04
Market

Why send a player to Poland at all

Poland is a stepping-stone market that actually works: an EU country, a televised top division, four professional levels beneath it, and clubs that still answer a well-prepared approach.

The pyramid we work in
  • EkstraklasaTop flight — UEFA places
  • Betclic 1. LigaLevel 2 — professional
  • 2. LigaLevel 3 — professional
  • 3. LigaLevel 4 — regional groups
  • CLJ U19 / U17Youth — national league

Club numbers and group formats change between seasons; we work from the current PZPN and league regulations, not from last year's table.

What it gives your player
  • An EU shop windowScouts from Germany, Austria, Belgium and the Netherlands watch Ekstraklasa and the 1. Liga. A good season here is seen.
  • A realistic first contractWages that a level-2 or level-3 club can genuinely pay, in a country where that salary is enough to live well.
  • Legal status in the Schengen areaA work permit and residence card in Poland change what is possible for a non-EU player's whole career.
  • Doors that still openSporting directors here take a phone call. That is worth more than a database subscription.

§06
Questions

What agents ask us first

Will you take my player away from me?
No. We work for the agent, not for the player. The representation contract stays with you, the player's contact stays with you, and our mandate is written so that we cannot sign him ourselves. It is the whole basis of the business — an agency that steals clients gets exactly one deal.
Are you licensed agents yourselves?
Best Way Plus provides marketing, organisational, administrative and relocation services. Where an action legally requires a licensed football agent, it is performed by licensed agents and by the law firm we cooperate with — and you are told in advance who is doing what and under which licence.
What level of player is realistic?
Honestly: 2. Liga and 3. Liga are open to a well-prepared unknown player. The 1. Liga wants either real minutes at a comparable level or a striking data profile. Ekstraklasa is a targeted, slow process. We tell you which of the three you are in before you buy a plane ticket.
Who pays for the trial?
Travel, accommodation and insurance are budgeted before departure and paid by the agent or the player, unless the club invites him at its own cost. We do not mark up those costs — you get the invoices.
Which languages do you work in?
Polish, English, Russian and Ukrainian in-house; Spanish, Serbian and Portuguese through partners. The player never negotiates in a language he does not control.
How fast can it go?
Inside an open window, with a player who fits, four to six weeks from first e-mail to registration is realistic. Anything faster is luck. Anything sold to you as faster is a warning sign.
