01Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you create an account or place an order we collect your name, email address, shipping address and order history. Visit counting is anonymous — a random identifier in a cookie, no cross-site tracking.

What we do with it. We use your data only to fulfil orders, provide receipts, and let you access your order history. We never sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Passwords. Stored only as salted scrypt hashes — we cannot see your password.

Cookies. One session cookie for visit statistics and your sign-in token stored in your own browser. No advertising cookies, no trackers.

Your rights. You may request a copy or complete deletion of your data at any time — see Contact.
