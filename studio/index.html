How is a custom site only $99?

We do one thing — one-page sites for local businesses — and we've streamlined every step. No meetings, no endless scope. That focus keeps the price low without cutting corners on design.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. We design first and send you a preview link. You pay the $99 once you approve it. Hosting billing starts the day your site goes live.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It's registered in your name. If you ever cancel, we transfer it to you and hand over your site files. No hostage situations.

Can I make changes later?

Yes — up to 30 minutes of edits every month are included: new hours, prices, photos, specials. Just email us. Bigger changes are quoted upfront.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Absolutely. We'll point it to your new site. Hosting is the same $12/month.

What do I need to provide?

Answers to a short questionnaire. A logo and photos help, but if you don't have them we use licensed stock images that fit your business.
