FAQ

Questions, answered.

How can a custom website cost only $99?

We focus on one thing — single-page sites for local businesses — and we've streamlined every step. No meetings, no endless scope. That efficiency lets us keep the price low without cutting corners on design.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. We build your design first and send you a preview link. You pay the $99 once you approve it. Hosting billing starts the day your site goes live.

Who owns the domain?

You do. We register it in your name. If you ever cancel, we'll transfer it to you and give you a copy of your site files — no hostage situations.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Your hosting plan includes up to 30 minutes of edits each month — new hours, prices, photos, specials. Just email us. Bigger changes are quoted upfront.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Absolutely. We'll point your existing domain to your new site. Hosting is the same $12/month.

What do I need to provide?

Just answers to a short questionnaire. Logo and photos help, but if you don't have them we'll use high-quality licensed stock images that fit your business.

What if I need more than one page?

Add extra pages for $49 each — Services, Menu, Gallery, About, whatever you need.
