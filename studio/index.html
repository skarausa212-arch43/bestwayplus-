FAQ

Fair
questions.

How is a custom site only $199?

We do one thing — one-page sites for local businesses — and we've streamlined every step. No meetings, no endless scope. That focus keeps the price low without cutting corners on design.

When do I pay?

At checkout, right after you fill out your site brief. Payment is handled by Stripe, so we never see your card. Your designer starts within one business day. Your .com domain and first month of hosting are free. After that, hosting is $12/month (or $119/year), and you can cancel anytime. Or you can choose your own hosting: we'll install your site there, with no monthly fee from us.

How does the online store work?

For +$200 we turn your site into a store: we list up to 20 of your products (photos, prices, sizes, colors), add a cart and checkout, and connect online payments to your own Stripe, Square or PayPal account. Money goes straight to you. The payment provider charges its usual processing fee per sale; we take no cut. Need more than 20 products? Just ask.

Is the domain really free?

Yes. Every website comes with a free .com domain registered in your name, plus your first month of hosting on us. From month two, hosting is $12/month (or $119/year), which also covers your domain renewal. Already have a domain? We connect it, and the first month of hosting is still free.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It's registered in your name. If you ever cancel, we transfer it to you and hand over your site files. No hostage situations.

Can I make changes later?

Yes — up to 30 minutes of edits every month are included: new hours, prices, photos, specials. Just email us. Bigger changes are quoted upfront.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Absolutely. We'll point it to your new site. Hosting is the same $12/month.

What do I need to provide?

Answers to a short questionnaire. A logo and photos help, but if you don't have them we use licensed stock images that fit your business.
